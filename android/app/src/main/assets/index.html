PROTECTION, WITH PERSPECTIVE

Find a hedge

Compare puts against your saved long HAL futures. Explore the cost of protection and how your current hedge measures up.

Budget is before selling existing puts. The loss limit applies to modelled scenarios, not every possible outcome. Protection runs through 15:30 IST on the chosen date.

Costs, liquidity & model assumptions

Defaults: 0.5% slippage per side, 10% spread limit, 6% interest and 0% dividend. These are editable assumptions. Quotes must be within 2 minutes, option/futures trades within 15 minutes, spot trade within 2 minutes, volume and OI positive, and top-of-book quantity sufficient for all units. Whole put lots must match futures units exactly.

How the shortlist is calculated

Lower cost uses the lowest total new-hedge cost. Stronger protection uses the smallest largest loss across the sampled scenarios. Balanced gives equal weight to cost and sampled loss after scaling each from 0 to 1 among qualifying puts, then minimises the sum of their squares. The same contract can win several categories. This is a transparent comparison, not a forecast or a probability score.

European Black–Scholes estimates put values using provider IV plus your change. Futures keep their current basis before expiry and settle at the scenario spot at expiry. Prices can differ from model values even when HAL is unchanged. Scenarios do not predict the price path, future volatility surface, fills, margin calls or physical settlement funding. Keep-current comparisons stop at the first existing put expiry. Review actual broker settlement obligations separately.

Net option premiums are already reflected in position P&L; they are not deducted again. Settlement cash entries are tracked separately and do not change strategy P&L.

Cash and margin are your broker estimates, not live balances. Cash-after-exit must already include futures settlement, old put proceeds and exit costs. Margin is entered per new futures lot; actual multi-leg margin may differ. Fees apply in full to each compared route.

Additional loss uses the current basket's quoted-bid liquidation value as baseline, before exit friction. Exact terminal payoff loss requires new futures and puts with matching expiry and exact units. It does not cap interim margin calls. Carry-point equivalents are higher entry exposure, not a separate cash debit. Original realised P&L and charges are retained.
